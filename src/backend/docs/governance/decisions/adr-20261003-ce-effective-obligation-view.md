# ADR: CE repair and final compilation share effective obligation identity facts

Accepted under approved unattended master plan; implementation active.

Fresh r07 repair explicitly allowed raw OBL-3 but final compilation removed its
ungranted root config and rejected the surviving INV-1 reference. Final scope
validation is correct; raw versus effective census is the implementation defect.

Factor actual CE normalization into a pure shared component. Existing Factory
committed-PM carrier supplies authenticated catalog/project-kind/command facts
through public CE boundaries. Projection returns normalized identity facts and
exclusions, not an executable completion contract or new grant. Provider context,
composer, early validator and final compilation agree on the same view. Depth
may be returned as repairable diagnosis while the final depth gate remains strict.

Do not choose the easier partial artifact roster/permanent missing-proof blocker:
qualified production already has the required carrier. Offline tasks-only callers
must report incomplete authority truthfully, never fabricate live parity. Preserve
optional/not_applicable identities, candidate/PM binding and typed correction.
No silent behavior deletion, scope expansion, model changes or target edits.

Cost: authenticated context plumbing across CE and Factory; scoped component,
no-I/O, real-carrier, regression and fresh qualification tests are mandatory.
